# Architecture

The platform is a local Streamlit application backed by synthetic CSV data and a small SQLite audit log.

## Data Flow

Synthetic customer, loan, transaction, and finance data is generated by `src.data.generate_synthetic_data`. The Streamlit app loads those datasets, applies scenario assumptions, and passes the data into reusable modules under `src/`.

## Module Dependencies

Credit risk produces PD, LGD, EAD, and expected loss. IFRS 9 uses those values for staging and provisions. Basel and IRB use exposure and capital assumptions to calculate RWA and capital ratios. The CRR3 lab adds output floor, CVA-lite, operational risk, and final Basel III reform impacts. ESG climate risk adjusts PD and LGD through transition and physical risk assumptions. XVA estimates simplified derivative valuation adjustments from expected positive exposure, counterparty PD, collateral, funding, and margin. COREP/FINREP-style reporting connects provisions to profit, retained earnings, CET1, and regulatory ratios. Reverse stress testing starts from a target CET1 depletion and identifies plausible transmission channels.

## Governance Wrapper

BCBS 239 controls, DORA operational resilience checks, third-party oversight, reconciliation, lineage, model inventory, EU AI Act-style controls, validation findings, drift checks, 1LOD/2LOD issue queues, and audit logs surround the risk calculations. This shows that regulatory reporting depends on controlled data, resilient operations, and governed models, not formulas alone.
