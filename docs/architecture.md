# Architecture

The platform is a local Streamlit application backed by synthetic CSV data and a small SQLite audit log.

## Data Flow

Synthetic customer, loan, transaction, and finance data is generated by `src.data.generate_synthetic_data`. The Streamlit app loads those datasets, applies scenario assumptions, and passes the data into reusable modules under `src/`.

## Module Dependencies

Credit risk produces PD, LGD, EAD, and expected loss. The model development lab trains baseline and challenger PD models, evaluates calibration and discrimination, assigns risk grades, and checks monitoring indicators. IFRS 9 uses those values for staging and provisions. The scenario ECL engine adds upside, baseline, and downside scenario weighting, lifetime PD approximation, stage migration, and an ECL bridge. Basel and IRB use exposure and capital assumptions to calculate RWA and capital ratios. The CRR3 lab adds output floor, CVA-lite, operational risk, and final Basel III reform impacts. ESG climate risk adjusts PD and LGD through transition and physical risk assumptions. XVA estimates simplified derivative valuation adjustments from expected positive exposure, counterparty PD, collateral, funding, and margin. COREP/FINREP-style reporting connects provisions to profit, retained earnings, CET1, and regulatory ratios. Reverse stress testing starts from a target CET1 depletion and identifies plausible transmission channels.

## Phase 3 Risk Engine Architecture

The core risk calculations are outside Streamlit page modules:

```text
Streamlit page
-> typed user input
-> domain risk engine
-> structured result with period rows, steps, assumptions and warnings
-> UI rendering
```

Key modules:

- `src/risk/validation.py`: shared range and consistency validation.
- `src/risk/scenarios.py`: economic scenarios and scenario-weight validation.
- `src/risk/pd.py`: direct PD, scenario-adjusted PD and PD term structures.
- `src/risk/lgd.py`: direct LGD, recovery-based LGD and downturn LGD.
- `src/risk/ead.py`: term-loan EAD, revolving EAD, amortising EAD and scenario-adjusted EAD.
- `src/risk/expected_loss.py`: point-in-time expected loss and discounted period-level lifetime ECL.
- `src/risk/ifrs9_staging.py`: educational IFRS 9 stage assignment with triggered rules.
- `src/risk/ifrs9.py`: 12-month, lifetime and scenario-weighted ECL.
- `src/risk/provision_bridge.py`: reconciled allowance movement bridge.
- `src/risk/capital_bridge.py`: simplified educational CET1 movement bridge.
- `src/risk/basel.py`: standardised RWA and capital ratios.
- `src/risk/irb.py`: simplified corporate IRB-style comparison with output-floor logic.
- `src/risk/stress_testing.py`: scenario-to-ECL-to-capital stress chain.

## Governance Wrapper

BCBS 239 controls, DORA operational resilience checks, third-party oversight, reconciliation, lineage, model inventory, EU AI Act-style controls, validation findings, drift checks, 1LOD/2LOD issue queues, and audit logs surround the risk calculations. This shows that regulatory reporting depends on controlled data, resilient operations, and governed models, not formulas alone.

## Phase 4 Governance Architecture

The governance layer now has typed domain objects for controls, control execution results, governance issues, workflow actions, evidence records and audit events.

```text
src/data/synthetic_governance.py
-> src/governance/data_quality.py
-> src/governance/issues.py
-> src/governance/lod_workflows.py
-> src/governance/audit.py
-> src/reporting/governance_reporting.py
-> Streamlit pages
```

The BCBS 239 page uses this chain to show source-data defects, control execution, failed-record samples, downstream impact, issue creation, 1LOD remediation, 2LOD challenge, 3LOD audit-review context, Risk-versus-Finance reconciliation, lineage and ownership.

The Executive Overview and COREP/FINREP-style page consume governance summaries so unresolved data-quality issues affect management narrative and report-production readiness.

## Phase 5 Model-Risk Architecture

The model-risk layer sits beside the Phase 3 risk engines and Phase 4 governance workflow:

```text
src/data/synthetic_model_risk.py
-> src/model_risk/inventory.py
-> src/model_risk/classification.py
-> src/model_risk/validation.py
-> src/model_risk/approvals.py
-> src/model_risk/monitoring.py
-> src/model_risk/drift.py
-> src/model_risk/reporting.py
-> Streamlit pages
```

Model-risk events can link to Phase 4 governance issues, restrictions and audit events. Credit Risk and IFRS 9 pages display model confidence so financial outputs are not shown without governance context.
