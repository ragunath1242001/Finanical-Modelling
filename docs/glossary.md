# Glossary

| Term | Full form / meaning | Project use |
|---|---|---|
| BCBS | Basel Committee on Banking Supervision | Source of BCBS 239 and Basel concepts. |
| BCBS 239 | Principles for effective risk data aggregation and risk reporting | Data-quality, lineage, reconciliation and issue workflow. |
| Basel III | International bank capital and liquidity framework | Capital ratios, RWA, leverage, LCR and NSFR. |
| IFRS | International Financial Reporting Standards | Accounting framework context. |
| IFRS 9 | Financial instruments accounting standard | Expected credit loss staging and provision examples. |
| ECL | Expected Credit Loss | `PD x LGD x EAD` and lifetime ECL. |
| PD | Probability of Default | Borrower default probability. |
| LGD | Loss Given Default | Loss severity after recoveries. |
| EAD | Exposure at Default | Exposure outstanding at default. |
| SICR | Significant Increase in Credit Risk | Stage 2 trigger concept. |
| CET1 | Common Equity Tier 1 | Highest-quality capital in the simplified capital bridge. |
| AT1 | Additional Tier 1 | Additional capital layer. |
| Tier 2 | Supplementary regulatory capital | Included in total capital ratio. |
| RWA | Risk-Weighted Assets | Exposure adjusted by risk weight. |
| LCR | Liquidity Coverage Ratio | HQLA divided by 30-day net cash outflows. |
| NSFR | Net Stable Funding Ratio | ASF divided by RSF. |
| HQLA | High Quality Liquid Assets | LCR numerator. |
| ASF | Available Stable Funding | NSFR numerator. |
| RSF | Required Stable Funding | NSFR denominator. |
| COREP | Common Reporting | Capital and prudential reporting concept. |
| FINREP | Financial Reporting | Financial statement reporting concept. |
| IRB | Internal Ratings-Based approach | Educational comparison to standardised RWA. |
| F-IRB | Foundation IRB | Bank estimates PD; other parameters may be prescribed. |
| A-IRB | Advanced IRB | More internally estimated parameters. |
| CCF | Credit Conversion Factor | Converts undrawn commitments into EAD. |
| EIR | Effective Interest Rate | Discounting concept for ECL. |
| PSI | Population Stability Index | Drift metric. |
| AUC | Area Under the ROC Curve | Ranking/discrimination metric. |
| Gini | `2 x AUC - 1` | Discrimination metric. |
| MRM | Model Risk Management | Inventory, validation, monitoring and restrictions. |
| 1LOD | First Line of Defence | Owns and manages risks. |
| 2LOD | Second Line of Defence | Provides independent oversight and challenge. |
| 3LOD | Third Line of Defence | Provides independent assurance. |
| AML | Anti-Money Laundering | Suspicious activity monitoring. |
| KYC | Know Your Customer | Customer-risk context. |
| DORA | Digital Operational Resilience Act | Operational resilience simulator. |
| XVA | Valuation Adjustments | CVA, DVA, FVA and MVA mini model. |
| CVA | Credit Valuation Adjustment | Counterparty credit risk valuation adjustment. |
| CRR3 | Capital Requirements Regulation 3 | Basel final reforms in EU context. |
| EBA | European Banking Authority | European regulatory context. |
| ECB | European Central Bank | European supervisory context. |
