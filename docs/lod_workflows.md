# 1LOD, 2LOD And 3LOD Workflows

## 1LOD

The First Line of Defence consists of business and operational functions that own, manage and are accountable for risks arising from their activities.

In the app, 1LOD can acknowledge an issue, investigate root cause, assess affected records, assess model/reporting impact, plan remediation, attach evidence and submit for 2LOD review.

## 2LOD

The Second Line of Defence consists of independent risk-management, compliance and governance functions that provide oversight, challenge, monitoring and guidance.

In the app, 2LOD can review severity, challenge impact analysis, review remediation, request stronger evidence, reject closure or accept closure.

## 3LOD

The Third Line of Defence provides independent assurance over the effectiveness of governance, risk management and internal controls.

The app includes a lightweight audit-review view over history and evidence.

## Closure

Material issue closure requires root cause, affected population, financial impact, remediation plan, preventive control, closure evidence and 2LOD acceptance.
